<h1>Error Liability Insurance: Protect Your Business from Costly Mistakes</h1>
<p><a href="https://unionbayrisk.com/business-insurance/errors-and-omissions-liability-insurance/">Error liability insurance</a>, commonly known as Errors and Omissions (E&amp;O) insurance, helps protect businesses and professionals against financial losses resulting from mistakes, oversights, negligence, or failure to deliver promised services. Professionals who provide advice, consulting, or specialized services may face claims from clients who believe an error has caused them financial harm. This coverage can help manage the legal and financial consequences of such situations.</p>
<p>Whether you operate a consulting firm, insurance agency, real estate business, or professional service company, having appropriate liability protection is an important part of managing business risks. Depending on the policy terms, error liability insurance may cover legal defense costs, settlements, and judgments associated with covered claims.</p>
<p>Choosing the right policy requires understanding your industry's specific risks, coverage limits, exclusions, and deductibles. Not every professional liability policy offers the same protection, so reviewing the details before purchasing is essential.</p>
